From Symbol to Understanding

The form of Śrī Gaṇeśa has been contemplated for generations as a visual language of wisdom. His elephant head, large ears, focused eyes, adaptable trunk, generous belly, broken tusk and many other symbols invite us to look beyond appearance and discover qualities that can be cultivated in human life.

This portal offers an independent digital journey through those themes — not as a replacement for the book, but as a space for reading, reflection and practice.
